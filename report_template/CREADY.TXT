---ELECTRONIC LETTERS ON COMPUTER VISION AND IMAGE ANALYSIS (ELCVIA)---
                    *** CAMERA READY VERSION  ***


When your article is accepted, ELCVIA Board will give you
the following information which you must fill to make 
the Camera  Ready version:


* LATEX 2e, LATEX 2.09 VERSIONS:

    1) Volume, Number, Initial and Final pages, Year

In the beginning of sample Latex Document you can find the
following instructions:
% INFORMATION TO FILL IN CAMERA READY VERSION
%-------------------------------------------------------------------
\newcommand{\vol}{0}            % Number of Volume (default 0)
\newcommand{\num}{0}            % Number (default 0)
\setcounter{page}{1}            % First page (default 1)
\newcommand{\begpag}{\thepage}  
\newcommand{\finpag}{7}         % Last Page
\newcommand{\yearp}{2000}       % Year of the issue (default 2000)
%-------------------------------------------------------------------


    2) Name of Associate Editor of your article (Initial surname and Name)

In the beginning of sample Latex Document you can find the
following instructions:
% INFORMATION TO FILL IN CAMERA READY VERSION
%--------------------------------------------------------------------
% name of Associate Editor given when article is accepted
\footnote[0]{\noindent Recommended for acceptance by $<$name$>$} 
%--------------------------------------------------------------------


    3) Received, revision (optional) and accepted dates.

In the beginning of sample Latex Document you can find the
following instructions:
% INFORMATION TO FILL IN CAMERA READY VERSION
%------------------------------------------------------------------
% Received, revised and accepted dates
\centerline{\small Received 1 January 2000; revised 1 January 2000; 
accepted 1 January 2000}

You must insert the name given by ELCVIA board in format initial surname
and name (example: E. Marti)



* WORD VERSION: You must change in .doc file

   1) Volume, Number, Initial and Final pages, Year

   2) Name of Associate Editor of your article (Initial surname and Name)
You must insert the name given by ELCVIA board in format initial surname
and name (example: E. Marti)

   3) Received, revision (optional) and accepted dates.


* LATEX and WORD versions:

You can insert some footnotes in the first page after the "Correspondence to"
footnote but before "Recommended for acceptance...".
